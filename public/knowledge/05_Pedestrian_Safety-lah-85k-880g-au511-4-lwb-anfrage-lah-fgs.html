Description of the project environment

Within the context of developing the [redacted-prj]/4CX_BL (E6L) battery-electric vehicles (BEVs), the vehicle front end must be developed analogously to the AU513/4EU_B (E6SB) project for partner protection in regard to meeting legal and consumer protection requirements for the Chinese market. The focus of the examinations is on evaluating the front end design for meeting the objectives with a passive hood, a China-specific vehicle front end with integrated sensors for highly automated driving, and an additional crash load level for the mobile progressive deformable barrier (MPDB) load case.

The initial basis of the examinations for analyzing styling versions will be existing vehicle models from the AU513/4EU_B project that must be adapted to the new project-specific conditions.

The requirements that must be met are described in [redacted-person] LAH.85K.880.F, [redacted-person] for [redacted-person] for [redacted-person], and are additionally based on [redacted]Specification LAH.85K.880.B and [redacted]LAH.85K.880.C, appendix 5. The latest version of the [redacted-person] always applies during the course of the project.

The contractor must prepare the "Anlage_01_FGS_Einzelanforderungen"appendix as per [redacted-person] Specification LAH.85K.880.F and keep it up to date during the course of the project. [redacted-co] will provide a template for this purpose.

In addition, the contractor must ensure that all required information from all tests and simulations is available so that it can be unambiguously allocated to the individual requirements from [redacted-person] Specification LAH.85K.880.F.

Project objective

The RFQ scope is subdivided, as per section 2.5.1, into fixed and optional parts. Please take this into account in the quotation by clearly contrasting the two options.

The objective of the examination is to develop a vehicle front end that is suitable for pedestrian protection, that is type-approval-capable as per the specifications in GTR 9 phase 2 and UN.R[redacted-phone]and all applicable national regulations, that makes the best possible contribution to a "5-star" rating as per the China NCAP consumer protection requirements in effect at the Start of Production (SOP), and gets a "good" rating for [redacted]Index (C-IASI) requirements. The front end design must be based on the [redacted](FlexPLI) and the new advanced pedestrian legform impactor (aPli) in all suspension heights.

Doubts regarding necessity or the action strategy for each country in which a pedestrian protection regulation is expected to come into effect during the vehicle's service life must be clarified – based on the best guess by the [redacted]Committee (KAK) – by the contractor before submitting its quotation.

The [redacted-prj]/4CX_BL (E6L) is developed as per [redacted-person] Process (PEP) 2.0. Within this context, the virtual development is given more weight. Accordingly, it is necessary to ensure, in this project, that the solutions are examined in terms of robustness regarding potential influencing variables with simulations. Within this context, relevant tolerances in the parts, the vehicle-empty positions, and the production process must be taken into account. In addition, the

robustness of the design for the known usual tolerances must be verified in real-life tests (dispersion of impactors, air-path angle, etc.).

The goals apply to the development to production readiness of all following project derivatives: E6L long wheelbase (LWB) China: [redacted-prj]/4CX_BL (E6L)

The requested packages are as follows:

  • Developing a vehicle front end with simulation and testing for the above objectives as a general development project

  • While carrying over the outer contour of base project AU513/4EU_B, with steel and air spring suspension system

  • While carrying over the exterior parts and the front end package known from the base project to the greatest extent possible

  • With new sensors to be integrated into the bumper cover; indirect lighting and illuminated rings and styling elements by

  • Determining the headform and legform test zone boundaries based on styling and class-A surfacing data in the various suspension heights (depending on the wheel/tire range and the suspension system)

  • Analyzing the existing deformation zones (cross sections with dimensional specifications; specification of critical positions) for styling, class-A surfacing, and design data

  • Developing and elaborating specifications (cross sections with dimensional specifications and areas and hard points) for influencing the test zone and its boundaries so that the aforementioned goals are met

  • Developing and elaborating proposals for influencing the parts of the bumper cover and of the package being integrated, as well as the corresponding brackets, connecting elements, wiring, tubes and hoses, etc., so that the aforementioned goals are met in the relevant test zones

  • Developing and elaborating proposals for influencing additional parts in the head test zone and of the package being influenced, incl. the corresponding brackets, connecting elements, wiring, tubes and hoses, etc., so that the aforementioned goals are met

  • Preparing and continuously updating "Anlage_01_FGS_Einzelanforderungen"

  • Preparing and elaborating a list of proposals for the series of tests in the Pre-[redacted-person] Vehicles (VFF) and [redacted-person] (PVS) phases all the way to type approval, as well as for the preliminary and primary testing of consumer protection, incl. the required time and parts.

Additionally requested packages include:

  • Analyzing and comparing the results and elaborating variants with, e.g., part and material changes in order to improve behavior so as to achieve the pedestrian protection goals

  • Formulating part requirements regarding dimensions, stiffness, and failure in the pedestrian protection impact area so as to meet the pedestrian protection goals

  • Setting up computable FE models on the basis of computer-aided design (CAD) data

  • Modifying these models in line with the current project-specific processing/styling/design version; within this context, the [redacted-person] numbering convention must be adhered to

  • Evaluating design solutions in the front end in regard to pedestrian protection requirements

  • Evaluating and summarizing the required simulation analyses with clear conclusions and statements on the required and recommended measures that cannot be misinterpreted

  • Evaluating and summarizing the series of tests with clear conclusions and statements on the required and recommended measures that cannot be misinterpreted

  • Elaborating and putting together a status report on all relevant load cases (regulations, C- NCAP, C-IASI) in order to prepare the relevant project milestone

  • Preparing release recommendations on the basis of the simulation and rest results and preparing the release documents
