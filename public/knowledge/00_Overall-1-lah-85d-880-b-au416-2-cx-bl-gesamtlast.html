Assumptions and basic conditions

  • Compliance with the legislation and government regulations in effect on the start date must be reliably guaranteed. Any anticipated stricter regulations beyond the start date, as well as important safety rating procedures (compliance at the forefront of the competition or "best in class," including potentially stricter requirements) must be taken into account. ([redacted-person] Safety department defines objectives on a case-by-case basis, if they are not described in this LAH.)

  • The developer must check which laws are pertinent for meeting the requirements for the parts described herein, up to and beyond the start date.

  • For safety-relevant parts that are intended as production carry-over parts (SERs) or carry-over part (COP) scopes, a vehicle-specific adaptation may need to be planned for.

For example:

  • Vent opening in the airbag for curtain airbag/side airbag

  • Airbag control module parameter tuning

  • Seat adaptation, etc.

  • In order to implement the scopes specified in the TPB, the respective [redacted-person] and [redacted-person] Specifications must be consulted, together with the supply specifications, test specifications, and procedural instructions cited herein.

  • The general objective of development is compliance with all legal limits and the fulfillment of all requirements in terms of voluntary commitment with a 20% safety margin; in other words, the pertinent laws must be fulfilled with certainty.

  • This serves as a safeguard against fluctuations in production (relating to joining technology, materials quality, materials strength) and test variance, and ensures the reliable fulfillment of all requirements (legal and voluntary) in series production throughout the production run.

Deviations from the development objective are possible in individual cases if the necessary robustness evaluation or quality checks during production are verified. However, they require the approval of the [redacted-person] department, if necessary in collaboration with the appropriate [redacted-person] departments.

  • [redacted]definitions must be taken into account.

  • Another objective is to pass the consumer tests as "best in class" among the competition. Appendix 5 to this LAH governs the details (document number LAH.85D.880.C).

  • The state of the art must be ensured until the vehicle's End of Production (EOP).

  • For the release, the specific documents must be completed as specified and archived. The associated simulation and testing data must be classified accordingly and documented.
