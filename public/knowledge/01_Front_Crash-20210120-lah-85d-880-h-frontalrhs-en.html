Description of the project environment

During development to production readiness, vehicle safety features and properties must be developed for the AU416_2CS_BL – Q6 etron long wheelbase (LWB) and AU416_3CS_BL – Q6 etron Sportback LWB.

The conceptual issues still open at the start of the project must be taken into account in the quotation.

The contract award comprises the development of the front restraint system for the 1st and 2nd row of seats

(in-position), as described in the [redacted-person] and applicable documents listed hereinafter.

The working model corresponds to that of a general developer award. The general developer is responsible for project coordination.

Project objectives

The objective of the project is robust fulfillment of the development goals for front occupant protection (in-position), while observing and achieving weight and cost targets.

The main task is the development of release-ready, robust systems and the associated release recommendations.
