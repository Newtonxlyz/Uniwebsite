Type-approval testing and certification

[A: BT-LAH[redacted-ext]]

The contractor must certify vehicle parts and genuine parts (e.g., spare parts, after-sales service parts) that are subject to component type approval and/or that require a conformity certificate (for radio transmission systems) as per international legislation, e.g., ECE, EC, INMETRO (Brazil), and CCC, regardless of the location of use/target market and vehicle model, and mark them with the corresponding identification as per the pertinent laws.

[A: BT-LAH[redacted-ext]]

For markets subject to obligatory type-approval name assignment and component type approval, the parts must be certified and marked using a type-approval name. The legal requirements for the Chinese market (CNCA-C11-01:2014) are specified in the Group standard [redacted-co]10511. If the area of application of a part ("type differentiation") must be indicated in the component type approval documents, this must be done exclusively using the "official type" and/or the shortened Group project code.

[A: BT-LAH[redacted-ext]]

The contractor must update the certificates before they expire or when pertinent laws are changed. The contractor must then submit the updated certificates to the purchaser in due time without the purchaser having to request them. This requirement applies over the entire product life cycle, including the period in which the legal obligation of supplying genuine parts applies.
