Seat belt arrangement concept design:

Packaging space examinations must be performed and the retractors, seat belt height adjusters, seat belt buckles, and belt guide fittings/seat belt guides, etc. must be positioned. The seat belt components must meet the specifications of the module package. The computer-aided design (CAD) data must always be checked to this effect.

For belt guide fittings/seat belt guides, brackets, recliners, and fasteners, not only the constructive design, but also the strength design (part simulation) must be performed as per [redacted-person] Specification for [redacted-person] of [redacted-person].

In addition, the installation of child seats and test objects must be ensured by means of CAD as per [redacted-person] for [redacted-person].

Regular consultations with the contact persons of the appropriate departments for the adjacent components and, if applicable, with others within the [redacted-person] will be required.

Provision must be made for a number of business trips.

Seat belt arrangement development to production readiness:

Implementation of the seat belt arrangement in a vehicle project in the development-to-production- readiness phase, from the [redacted-person] Decision through to Start of Production (SOP) of the project

+ 3 months.

Packaging space examinations must be performed and the retractors, seat belt height adjusters, seat belt buckles, and belt guide fittings/seat belt guides, etc. must be positioned. The seat belt components must meet the specifications of the module package. CAD data must always be checked to this effect.

For belt guide fittings/seat belt guides, brackets, recliners, and fasteners, not only the constructive design, but also the strength design (part simulation) must be performed as per [redacted-person] Specification for [redacted-person] of [redacted-person] (LAH.DUM.857.BB).

In addition, the installation of child seats and test objects must be ensured by means of CAD as per [redacted-person] for [redacted-person].

Regular consultations with the contact persons of the appropriate departments for the adjacent components and, if applicable, with others within the [redacted-person] will be required.

Provision must be made for a number of business trips.

Seat belt design:

The constructive design and positioning of components of the seat belt system parts in the vehicle environment (e.g., seat belt guides, seat belt buckle holders) takes place, in consultation with [redacted]in accordance with the project phases listed in sections 2.1.2 and 2.1.3.

The belt guide fittings/seat belt guides are designed as per [redacted-person] for [redacted]Guide and Front B-[redacted]The contractor is responsible for the part design and part strength, as well as the component tests.

Data sets, part equipment lists, and drawings (individual parts and entire scopes of supply) must be created and archived.

Design FMEA:

Creating and tracking a system failure modes and effects analysis (FMEA) for the arrangement of the seat belt components in a vehicle project.

The APIS IQ-FMEA-L system must be used for this purpose.

A team must be assembled with the responsible contacts of the involved departments, as well as, e.g., [redacted]and, if necessary, the appropriate component suppliers, to work through the FMEA forms at regular meetings.

The progress of the FMEA must be indicated in regular status reports and completed at the deadlines specified in the project milestones.

Part application development:

For the seat belt systems in a vehicle project, the functions and properties of the components must be developed taking into account their interaction with the environment. This includes the following parts: retractors, webbing, latch plates, belt guide fittings, height adjusters, belt end fittings, lap belt pretensioners, seat belt guides, and seat belt buckles.

Direct consultation with the suppliers and [redacted]is required for this purpose.

Provision must be made for a number of business trips.

Function in the vehicle:

The functions of the seat belt system components and actions from the arrangement FMEA in the vehicle must be checked. This includes acoustic characteristics (noise, vibration, and harshness [NVH]), wearing comfort, seat belt retraction, and child seat/test object installation in the vehicle.

Regular meetings must be organized and held with the seat belt system suppliers on the vehicle ("[redacted-person]") to jointly evaluate and define actions for the aforementioned aspects in the vehicle.

It must be ensured that the current part versions are installed in test and acceptance vehicles and that the identified faults are rectified.

The assembly BOM for prototypes and pre-production vehicles must be completed.
