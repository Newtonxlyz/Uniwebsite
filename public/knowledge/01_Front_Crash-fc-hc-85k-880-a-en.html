Description of the project environment

During development to production readiness, vehicle safety features and properties must be developed for the eQ5 long wheelbase (LWB) ([redacted-prj]/2CX_BL) and eQ6 LWB ([redacted-prj]/3CX_BL). Variance in terms of drive layout, energy storage, and equipment must be taken into account as per current [redacted-person] Book (TPB). The requirement is to develop frontal/rear crash properties, including electrical safety and crash response, as described in the following [redacted-person].

Project objectives

The objective of the project is robust fulfillment of the frontal/rear crash development goals, including electrical safety, at minimum cost and weight. The main task is the development of release- ready systems and the associated release recommendations.

The laws to be complied with and the consumer protection targets to be achieved must be taken from the [redacted]Specification and its appendices.

All load cases that are not internal [redacted-co] load cases must be processed in accordance with the consumer protection test protocols and laws currently in effect.

Vehicle safety CAD/DMU examinations for frontal and rear-crash protection

The objective is to produce test results for the respective model phases at the component level and in the whole vehicle environment, comparative studies, and computer-aided design (CAD) drawings of crash-suitable parts. [redacted-person] LAH.DUM.880.ED describes the services to be rendered and the requirements in detail.

Frontal/rear crash engineering

The objective of the project is to plan, prepare, conduct, and evaluate the whole-vehicle frontal and rear crash tests, as described in LAH.DUM.880.FJ. The contractor is provided with an initial test plan as part of the RFQ [redacted-person]. This test plan includes the number of tests, the test configurations, and the location of test performance.

The aim is to compare the test results with reference to the [redacted-person] requirements and to issue a release recommendation for the frontal/rear crash properties at the respective milestones. In the event of non-compliance with the specifications, appropriate solutions must be devised.
