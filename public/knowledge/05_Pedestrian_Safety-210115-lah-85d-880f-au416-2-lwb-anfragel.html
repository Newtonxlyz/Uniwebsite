Project-specific contents of the order

Description of the project environment

In the course of development to production readiness of the battery electric vehicles (BEVs) [redacted-prj]/2CX_BL (eQ5L) and [redacted-prj]/3CX_BL (eQ6L), the vehicle front end must developed analogously to project [redacted-prj]/2EU_B with regard to partner protection and fulfillment of the legal and consumer protection requirements for the Chinese market. The examinations focus on evaluating the front-end concept for goal fulfillment with a passive hood, a China-specific vehicle front end with integrated sensors for highly automated driving, and an additional crash load level for the mobile progressive deformable barrier (MPDB) load case.

The starting point for examinations to analyze design version are existing models from project [redacted-prj]/2EU_B, which must be adapted to the new project-specific circumstances.

The requirements to be met are described in [redacted-person] LAH.85D.880.G "[redacted]for [redacted-person]" and are also based on whole vehicle safety [redacted-person] LAH.85D.880.C, appendix 5. The latest version of the [redacted-person] always applies during the course of the project.

The contractor must prepare appendix "AU416_2_LWB_Anlage_01_Einzelanforderungen" (AU416_2_LWB_appendix_01_individual requirements) as per [redacted-person] Specification LAH.85D.880.G and keep it up to date during the course of the project. [redacted-co] will provide a template for this purpose.

In addition, the contractor must ensure that all the necessary information from all tests and simulations is available, so that these can be assigned unambiguously to the individual requirements from [redacted-person] Specification LAH.85D.880.G.

Project objectives

Please note that the scope of the RFQ is subdivided as per section 2.5.1 into fixed and optional elements. Please take this into account in the quotation by providing a clear comparison of the two options.

The aim of the examination is to develop a vehicle front end suitable for pedestrian protection, which is capable of type approval as per [redacted-person] Regulation (GTR) 9 Phase 2 and UN Regulation R[redacted-phone] In addition, the front end to be developed must make the best possible contribution to a 5-star rating as per the consumer protection requirements of China NCAP (C- NCAP) in effect at Start of Production (SOP) and a rating of "good" with regard to the [redacted]Index (C-IASI) requirements. The front end must be designed with the flexible pedestrian legform impactor (FlexPLI) and the new advanced pedestrian legform impactor (aPli) in all suspension heights.

Ambiguities regarding the necessity or the action strategy for each country that is expected to introduce a pedestrian protection law during the vehicle's production run must be clarified by the contractor before submitting the quotation, based on the best guess of the [redacted]Committee (KAK).

The [redacted-prj]/2CX_BL (eQ5L) and [redacted-prj]/3CX_BL (eQ6L) will be developed in accordance with [redacted-person] Process (PEP) 2.0. A greater emphasis is placed on virtual development in this process. Therefore, it must be ensured in the project that the concepts are examined by simulation with regard to their robustness to potential influencing factors. Pertinent tolerances of the parts, of the vehicle levels, and from the production process must also be taken into account. Likewise, the robustness of the design to the known, customary tolerances must be verified in actual tests (impactor variance, impact angle, and the like).

The goals apply to the development to production readiness of all the following project derivatives: eQ5 long wheelbase (LWB) China: [redacted-prj]/2CX_BL (eQ5L)

eQ6 LWB China: [redacted-prj]/3CX_BL (eQ6L)

The scopes requested for quotation are as follows:

  • Development of a vehicle front end by simulation and testing for the aforementioned objectives as a general development project

  • With adoption of the outer contour of the [redacted-prj]/2EU_B basic project with steel and air-spring suspension

  • With greatest possible adoption of the exterior parts and the front-end package from the basic project

  • With new sensors and styling elements to be integrated in the bumper cover Through

  • Determining the head and leg test zone limits based on styling and class-A surfacing data in the various suspension heights (depending on rim/tire program and suspension)

  • Analyzing the existing deformation zones (dimensioned cross sections, specification of critical positions) based on styling, class-A surfacing, and design data

  • Developing and finalizing specifications (dimensioned cross sections with surfaces and hard points) to influence the test zone and its limits for meeting the aforementioned goals

  • Developing and finalizing proposals to influence the components of the bumper cover and the package to be integrated, as well as its brackets, connecting elements, cabling, hoses, etc., for meeting the aforementioned goals in the pertinent test areas

  • Developing and finalizing proposals to influence other parts in the head test area and of the package to be influenced, as well as its brackets, connecting elements, cabling, hoses, etc., for meeting the aforementioned goals

  • Creating and continuously updating appendix "AU416_2_LWB_Anlage_01_Einzelanforderungen"

  • Developing and finalizing a suggestion list for the test series in the Pre-[redacted-person] Vehicles (VFF) and [redacted-person] (PVS) phases through to type approval, as well as for the preliminary and main consumer protection testing, including the time and parts required

Furthermore, the scopes requested for quotation include:

  • Adopting, supplementing, completing finite element (FE) models from the basic project [redacted-prj]/2EU_B to perform the necessary simulations for head, leg, and hip impact

  • Analyzing and comparing the results and developing variants by means of, e.g., part or material changes to improve the behavior and thereby achieve the pedestrian protection objectives

  • Formulating part requirements in terms of dimensions, stiffness, or failure in the pedestrian protection impact area in order to meet the pedestrian protection requirements

  • Configuring computable FE models based on computer-aided design (CAD) data

  • Adapting these models to the project-specific current processing/styling/design version, whereby the [redacted-person] numbering convention must be adhered to

  • Evaluating design solutions in the front end in terms of pedestrian protection requirements

  • Evaluating and summarizing the necessary simulation analyses with clear, unambiguous conclusions and statements regarding the required or recommended actions

  • Evaluating and summarizing the test series analyses with clear, unambiguous conclusions and statements regarding the required or recommended actions

  • Developing and compiling a status report on all pertinent load cases (legal, C-NCAP, C-IASI) to prepare the respective project milestone

  • Creating release recommendations based on simulation and test results and preparing/creating the release documents

  • Preparing, supporting, and evaluating the tests (see also "Testing performance description")

  • Comparing with the basic project and transferring empirical values from the basic project

  • Handing over a validated production simulation model upon project conclusion as well as a final report, including documentation of lessons learned
