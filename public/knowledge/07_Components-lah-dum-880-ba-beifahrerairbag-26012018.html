Brief description of the scope of development

A front passenger airbag module must be developed.

Objectives

A front passenger airbag module must be developed that complies with all current and expected legislation in the target markets and meets all the requirements in this Group BT- LAH. All specified deadlines must be met.

This BT-LAH and all the listed applicable documents define the technical data and basic conditions for the scope of services to be described for the front passenger airbag and dashboard. The front passenger airbag system comprises the complete airbag module and its proper interaction within the whole vehicle.

The system supplier must make suggestions regarding product improvement. In particular, the production supplier must discuss with the appropriate departments the possibilities of using new materials and methods to improve vehicle safety and to reduce weight.

  1. Gas generator

General information

The gas generator must be capable of ensuring the filling volume (airbag volume) required by the system, while adhering to the necessary inflation times.

The power, power characteristics, and generator type must be agreed upon with the purchaser's [redacted-person] department with reference to in-position and OOP.

Any potential conflict in objectives between OOP and in-position requirements must be minimized through the implementation of suitable solutions.

In addition to the functional requirements, the environmental requirements must also be observed.

The objective is to develop a gas generator that does not emit any particles that could cause damage to the airbag material. This applies over the entire temperature range and generator characteristic map.

The contractor must verify that the gas generator can be adjusted in terms of its power and power characteristics (e.g., higher/lower charge).

By accepting the order, the contractor confirms that all power variants of the generator can be used in a fully functional module.

If necessary, tank pressure curves with an appropriate representation of the onset behavior must be provided to the purchaser in the course of development.

If the hold time or power of the airbag module is not sufficient for the restraining effect, the purchaser may demand subsequent improvement free of charge. The purchaser will evaluate this. The objective is to achieve constant gas generator characteristics for the entire filling range.

The development goal is an odorless, clean, and smokeless gas generator. Glowing of the airbag is not permissible. The pyrotechnic properties of the propellant must not change over the service life. Only legally approved propellants may be used.

The gas generator technology must be agreed upon with the purchaser. Only propellants that do not contain any phase-stabilized ammonium nitrate (PSAN) are permissible.

The design must follow [redacted-co]82513. A small fluctuation range is one of the quality criteria for the gas generator.
