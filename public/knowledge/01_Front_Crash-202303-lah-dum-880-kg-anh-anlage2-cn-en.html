General points:

The quality of the following criteria must be evaluated in consultation with [redacted-person]:

In crash tests and crash simulations with an offset deformable barrier (ODB) at 64 km/h and worst-case engine type (structural integrity load case), the structural integrity (incl. evaluation of load paths, e.g., based on the "structure catalog") must be ensured in addition to fulfillment of the structure value table (see above). This load case must validate the structural behavior of the platform and vehicle hat.

The requirements for the dashboard carrier are described in detail in the current [redacted-person] from [redacted-person] (e.g., component tests). The requirement from [redacted-person] is that the dashboard carrier must not tear, although incipient cracks are permissible. The connection between A-pillars must be maintained. The requirements apply to all crash load cases listed in sections a) and b)

After a crash, it must be possible for at least one door per seat row to be opened from the outside without tools. This applies to all statutory and

consumer-protection load cases and the following internal load cases: FMVSS 301 (old) rear impact, small overlap (SOL) worldwide, pole frontal impact up to 35 km/h, driving under truck at 30 km/h, ODB must-fire thresholds, 30° and solid wall.

High-voltage [redacted-person] LAH.DUM.880.AS also applies to electrified vehicles.

Robustness in SOL ([redacted]Index [C-IASI] CN) load case:

The scatter band of the dispersion computations with crash weight + 5% from C-IASI crash weight (minimum number of dispersion computations is defined in the project) must lie below the limit curve from "good" to "acceptable."

Requirements for windshield strength:

The bond between windshield and windshield frame must be maintained after a crash in all load cases and with every engine variant

The following benchmark applies in the event of windshield detachment:

The windshield must not detach by more than 25% with regard to the total perimeter of the windshield

It must be ensured that the area of the windshield base/plenum panel is not negatively affected by an unfavorable package of parts (e.g., windshield wiper linkage, electronic control units, etc.)
